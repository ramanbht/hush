Hush settings

 

Filter lists

Lists decide which requests are blocked. Turning one off takes effect immediately.

    Page cleanup

    • Hide ad elements
      Removes the empty boxes and banners that blocked ads leave behind.
    • Patch anti-adblock and in-page ads
      Runs small fixes from the uBlock lists on sites that need them, such as YouTube.

    These apply to pages you open or reload after changing them.
